About Us

Discover our history, vision, and values that shape tomorrow’s leaders

Welcome to BTHSS

Established in 1969, Bangalore Town Higher Secondary School has been a beacon of academic excellence and holistic education. Our school is committed to nurturing young minds through innovative teaching, modern facilities, and a focus on values.

Over the decades, BTHSS has built a reputation for outstanding results, vibrant extracurricular activities, and a community that encourages leadership and responsibility.

  • Experienced and dedicated faculty
  • Strong academic track record
  • Focus on co-curricular and character building

Our Vision

To inspire and empower students to become responsible global citizens through knowledge, values, and innovation.

Our Mission

To provide a supportive learning environment that fosters creativity, critical thinking, and leadership skills in every child.

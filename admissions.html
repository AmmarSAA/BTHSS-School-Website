Admission Process

Eligibility

Admissions are open for classes I to XII. Students must meet the age criteria and academic requirements set by the board.

  • Class I – Minimum age: 6 years
  • Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Previous year’s report card

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Records
  • Passport size photographs
  • Address Proof of Parents
